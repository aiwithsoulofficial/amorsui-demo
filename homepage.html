Charm Sarical, RN

Before aesthetics, Charm spent over a decade working in ICU and Emergency nursing - environments where precision, care under pressure, and genuine empathy aren't optional. That clinical foundation shapes everything she does in the clinic today.

She made a deliberate choice: non-surgical only. You should never have to go under the knife to feel like yourself again. Every treatment is TGA approved, and all prescription items are prescribed through telehealth doctors for your safety.
