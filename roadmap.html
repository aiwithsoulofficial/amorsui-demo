The Opportunity

You have the skills and the style. Your website isn't converting them into bookings.

01

Your booking system is broken

Every "Book Now" button leads to a Timely error page. New clients literally cannot book online right now. Every day this continues is lost revenue.

02

Treatment names don't mean anything to your clients

PDO Threads, SQT Bio-Microneedling, Cosmetic Enhancers — these are industry terms. Your ideal client is searching for "how to look younger without surgery", not a treatment code.

03

No results = no proof

There are zero before and after photos anywhere on your website or gallery. Your clients are seeing claims but no evidence. Social proof is the #1 conversion driver in aesthetics.

04

No way to capture interested visitors

Your only CTA is a broken Book Now button. Anyone browsing who isn't ready to book has no way to stay connected — no quiz, no consultation offer, no email capture.

The Plan

Four phases. Visible results at every stage.

Phase 1 Week 1

Immediate Fixes

  • Fix your Timely booking integration so clients can actually book
  • Fix your phone number (currently linking to the wrong number)
  • Problem-focused landing page with consultation as the first step
  • Start capturing before and after photos from every treatment — this becomes your most powerful marketing asset
Phase 2 Weeks 2–3

Conversion System

  • Full homepage rewrite — problem-first messaging that speaks your client's language
  • Skin concern assessment quiz that captures leads 24/7
  • Individual treatment pages optimised for "non-surgical facelift Tarneit", "skin rejuvenation Wyndham"
  • AI search visibility so ChatGPT and Perplexity recommend you
  • Google Business Profile setup and review collection
Phase 3 Weeks 3–4

Aria Voice Agent

  • Every call answered — even when you're mid-treatment
  • After-hours bookings captured automatically
  • Appointment confirmations and reminders to reduce no-shows
  • You focus on clients, Aria handles the phones
Phase 4 Month 2+

Paid Growth

  • Targeted ads across Tarneit, Truganina, Werribee, Point Cook corridor
  • Problem-focused ad creative using your real before and after photos — by now you'll have them
  • Retargeting quiz completers who showed interest but didn't book
  • Low competition in Wyndham means stronger returns on every dollar
